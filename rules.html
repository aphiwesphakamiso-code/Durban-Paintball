The Rules

KNOW YOUR
RULES
BEFORE YOU PLAY

When your group arrives we will go over all the rules and how the equipment works — but please read these before your event. Safety is our absolute priority.

NEVER REMOVE YOUR GOGGLES IN THE PLAY AREA

Removing goggles while on the field can cause serious eye injury. This is the single most important rule — no exceptions, ever.

Safety Rules

PAINTBALL & SPLATBALL RULES

01

Never Remove Your Goggles Critical

Goggles must remain on at all times while in the play area. Removing them can cause serious, potentially permanent eye injury.

02

Age Limit: 10 Years & Older

The minimum age to participate is strictly 10 years old. No exceptions will be made regardless of circumstances.

03

Played Entirely at Own Risk

Paintball and Splatball are played entirely at participants' own risk. Ensure all players are aware and have consented before joining.

04

Closed Shoes Must Be Worn

Tekkies or running shoes are ideal. No sandals, flip-flops or open shoes are permitted on the field at any time.

05

No Shooting at Close Range

Shooting at close range is strictly prohibited. Always maintain a safe shooting distance from other players at all times.

06

No Shooting in the Safety Area

The safety and rules area is a designated no-shoot zone. All guns must be barrel-down with the safety on in this area.

07

Guns Stay in the Safety Area

Paintball guns are not permitted outside the designated safety area under any circumstances.

08

Strictly No Alcohol Enforced

No alcohol is allowed before or during your games. Anyone suspected of being intoxicated will not be permitted to play.

What to Bring & Wear
  • Closed shoes (tekkies or running shoes)
  • Old, comfortable clothes you don't mind getting marked
  • Drinking water and snacks
  • Sunscreen (outdoor field)
  • No alcohol before or during play
  • No sandals or open-toed shoes
10+

AGE LIMIT

All participants must be a minimum of 10 years old. Applies to Paintball, Splatball and Lasertag.
