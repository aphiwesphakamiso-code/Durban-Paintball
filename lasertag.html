What Is It?

COMBAT SIM,

Additional Game Device

SIRIUS STATION

The Sirius Station is an additional device added to the game to enhance your experience. It can be set to serve a number of different purposes, each affecting the game mode at play.

R

Respawn Point

Set individually for each team. Touch the station to respawn back into the battle.

B

Base

Respawns friendly players and deals damage to enemies who enter the zone.

M

Medikit

Slowly replenishes your health units when nearby. Protect it or it becomes your enemy's lifeline.

X

Radiation Zone

Damages the health units of any player within the zone. Control it, avoid it, or use it as a trap.

F

Flag

Used with Capture the Flag game modes — coordinate your team to capture and defend.
